What I work on

My training covers research integrity, technical skills and professional development. Through coaching and mediation, I support researchers in navigating difficult situations and addressing conflict.

Research background

I studied psychology and molecular biology at the University of Graz and completed a PhD in brain imaging at Cardiff University. I spent eight years in postdoctoral research at Cardiff and the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences, and worked as a data scientist at a biotech startup.
